A Boolean search string is a structured query that uses logic operators — AND, OR, and NOT — to narrow or broaden search results. Recruiters have used them for years to find candidates precisely. This tool lets you use the same technique to find jobs and opportunities that standard search filters miss entirely.
Yes — Boolean search works on LinkedIn's free version. Simply paste the generated string into LinkedIn's main search bar and press Enter. You don't need LinkedIn Premium or Recruiter to benefit from this.
LinkedIn's filters are broad and limited — they match on a small set of predefined fields. Boolean search scans the full text of profiles and job listings, including headlines, summaries, and experience sections.
